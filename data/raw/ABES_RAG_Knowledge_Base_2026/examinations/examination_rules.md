# ABES Examination Knowledge
The official ABES circulars page publishes examination forms, examination schedules/guidelines, challenge evaluation, branch-change and other academic notices.

Official source: https://www.abes.ac.in/circulars.html

RAG rule:
For a date/deadline question, retrieve the latest applicable official circular. If two notices conflict, prefer the latest official notice applicable to the student's program/year. If applicability is uncertain, ask for program/year or route to the Examination Cell.
